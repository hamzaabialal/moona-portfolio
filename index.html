About

Eight years of making complicated software easier to use.

Since 2018 I’ve designed software for small teams in analytics, finance, health and developer tools. Most projects start with a workflow everyone agrees is painful and nobody has had time to fix.

I cover the whole path: talking to users, sketching flows, designing in Figma and building the production front end. Because one person does both, nothing gets lost between the mockup and what ships.
